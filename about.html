Our story

We did not start with a system. We started by doing every part of cold email by hand, and building the system was the whole point.

We started by doing everything manually

Every campaign in the early days was hand work. We wrote each email ourselves, hunted down each lead, and sent everything by hand. It taught us what actually gets a reply and what gets ignored, but it did not scale, and we were spending hours on work a machine should do.

So we built a system, and a skill that holds what we learned

After running a lot of campaigns, we stopped guessing. We turned what worked into a system. The core of it is a copywriting skill that carries our knowledge and writes high-performing copy the way we would, on every account, without us starting from a blank page each time.

We fixed the leads

For a long time we did what most people do: we bought leads from old databases and vendors. The data was stale and it bounced. Now we source up-to-date leads of whatever kind we need, fresh and verified, matched to the exact person a client wants to reach. No more paying for a list that is three years out of date.

Now we are adding AI to the whole sales motion

The system keeps growing. We build AI into the parts that used to eat our time: a reply handler that drafts responses in the right voice, a research brief that fires before every booked call so you walk in prepared, and full systems we build and hand off to a client's own team. The goal is the same as day one, more booked calls with less manual work.
