The Mechanism

Four channels, not one.

One channel gets ignored. Four channels hitting the same named list in the same week, all starting on the same day, is why the number is 20 and not 5. Nothing goes out in a trickle while the rest is still being built. Every touch points at the same people, in the same order, on purpose.

How It Works

Fourteen days to build it. Then everything starts at once.

Most outbound starts the week it is bought and limps for a month while the pieces catch up. We do it the other way round. The first two weeks are spent building the offer with you and standing up all four channels in parallel, and nothing is sent to a stranger until every piece is tested. On day 14 it all goes live together, and that is when your 28 days start.

The Model

An SDR hire costs you a salary from day one, three months of ramp, and the cost of doing it again when they leave. This costs you nothing until a call is actually on the calendar.
